# Model card: flex-extinction-0.2

Generated by `python main.py train export --config configs/training/flex_extinction.toml`.

## Intended use

Estimate, for the simulator and the chatbot, how early the visible flame of a burning fuel droplet goes out in a given microgravity atmosphere, as the droplet diameter at extinction divided by its initial diameter. A ratio near 1 means the flame barely outlived ignition; a small ratio means the droplet burned almost completely. It supports engineering judgment and does not replace material certification.

Outputs: `extinction_diameter_ratio`, `extinction_diameter_mm`, `in_tested_range`, `out_of_range_fields`, `support_n`, `nearest_tests`, `confidence`, `model_version`, `training_source`.

## Limitations

- Conditional model: it describes the tests whose visible flame went out. Whether the flame goes out at all is the question of flex-outcome, and this model must be read together with it (decision D-08).
- Microgravity only: gravity is not an input, and the prediction must not change with any gravity control.
- Extinction means extinction of the visible flame. Heptane droplets can keep burning as a dim cool flame after the visible flame goes out, so a heptane extinction diameter is not the end of all burning.
- Extinction diameters were measured only while the droplet stayed in the camera view. Tests without a measured diameter are excluded, and the diameters that appear only in the TestSheets are not used (decision D-04).
- Pressure was tested at two levels only (see the coverage facts). The per-input limits accept any pressure between the two levels, and the joint range check only rejects pressures far from both of them.
- Suppressants (CO2, He) were tested only at moderate oxygen levels. No flame went out in the Moon-like region, so this model has no training rows there.
- Fiber-supported tests are used for training, but every prediction is made for a free droplet.
- Tests above the pressure limit are excluded: there, pressure is confounded with a single CO2 mixture.
- Predictions are clipped to the physical range of the ratio.
